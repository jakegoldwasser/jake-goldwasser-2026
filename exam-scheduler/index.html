Fill the Testing Center Form

The Testing Center's form takes one sitting at a time. Each opens the real form in a new tab with its answers filled in: check it, attach the test on the last page and press Submit, then come back for the next. Submit each before opening the next (an unsent form's draft replaces the next one's answers; if a form shows the wrong student, click Clear form at its foot and open it again from here).
