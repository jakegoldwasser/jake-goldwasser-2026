Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

The short version

  • BookBug uses your Google account only to sign you in and save your books.
  • It only reads a Google Doc when you pick that file yourself. It can’t see anything else in your Drive.
  • There are no ads and no analytics, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.
  • You can delete your data whenever you like.

Who we are

BookBug (jake-goldwasser.com/bookbug) is a free tool, made by Jake Goldwasser, for turning a Google Doc of entries into a print-ready PDF book. In this policy, “BookBug,” “we,” and “us” mean the tool and the person who runs it.

Information we collect

Your Google account. When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, name, and email address. The ID tells us which saved books are yours. Your name fills in the “author” field on a new book, and it and your email appear in your sign-in session so the page can show who is signed in.

Google Docs you choose to import. If you import from Google Drive, BookBug asks for the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. You pick a document in Google’s own file picker, and that permission only covers files you pick. Your browser then reads that one document’s text and turns it into book entries. BookBug can’t list, read, change, or delete anything else in your Drive, and it never writes to your Drive.

Your books. Your book titles, entries, sections, formatting settings, and trash are saved in your browser’s local storage as you work. When you’re signed in, they are also saved to your BookBug account so you can open them on other devices.

We don’t collect your contacts, location, payment information, or anything else from your Google account.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in (a sign-in session lasts up to 30 days).
  • To save your books and load them again on any device where you sign in.
  • To bring the text of a Google Doc you picked into a book.

That’s all. We don’t use your information for advertising, profiling, or training AI or machine-learning models, and no person reads your books unless you ask us to help with a problem.

Google user data

BookBug’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, we:

  • use Google user data only to provide the features you see in BookBug;
  • don’t transfer it to anyone else, except as needed to run the service (see “Where your data is stored”), to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you;
  • don’t use it for advertising, and don’t sell it;
  • don’t let any person read it unless you agree, it’s needed for security or to comply with the law, or it has been anonymized.

Where your data is stored

Saved books are stored in a database on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the United States, keyed to your Google account ID. Your Google password is never sent to BookBug, because sign-in happens entirely on Google’s side. Google Doc imports go straight from Google to your browser. They don’t pass through our servers, though an imported entry is saved with the rest of your book like anything else you write.

The same sign-in and storage also serve other book tools on jake-goldwasser.com. Each tool keeps its own data separate within your account.

BookBug loads fonts from Google Fonts and uses Google’s sign-in and file-picker scripts. Google handles those requests under the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We don’t sell, rent, or share your information with anyone. The only exceptions are the service providers named above (Google and AWS), which run the service for us, and cases where the law requires it.

Cookies and local storage

BookBug uses your browser’s local storage to keep your work in progress, your sign-in session, and your preferences. It sets no advertising or tracking cookies. Google’s sign-in may set its own cookies.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your saved books stay in your account until you delete them or ask us to delete your account.

  • Delete a book: delete it in BookBug. It goes to the trash and is permanently removed from your account after 7 days, or right away if you delete it from the trash.
  • Delete everything: email jake.goldwasser@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. We’ll delete all your stored data within 30 days and let you know when it’s done.
  • Remove BookBug’s access to Google: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time.
  • Clear your browser’s copy: sign out, or clear this site’s data in your browser.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. The saving service accepts only verified Google sign-ins or sessions it signed itself. Google Drive access tokens stay in your browser’s memory and expire within an hour. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we hold to the minimum the tool needs.

Children

BookBug isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has signed up, contact us and we’ll delete their data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update it here and change the date at the top. If a change affects how we use Google user data, we’ll ask for your consent first.

Contact

Questions or requests: jake.goldwasser@gmail.com.
